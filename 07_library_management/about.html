About the Library

The Central Library was established in 1995 along with the college. It has a collection of more than 45,000 books, 120 national and international journals, e-books and a digital library. The library is fully automated and uses a barcode-based issue and return system.

Facilities

  • Reading hall with seating for 200 students
  • Reference section and back-volume journals
  • Digital library with 30 computers and internet
  • Book bank facility for SC/ST and economically weaker students
  • Reprography (photocopy) service

Library Rules

  1. Students may borrow up to 3 books for 14 days.
  2. A fine of ₹2 per day is charged for late return.
  3. Reference books and journals cannot be taken out.
  4. Maintain silence inside the library.
