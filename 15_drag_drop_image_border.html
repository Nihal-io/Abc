Drag and Drop + Repeating Image Border

1. Drag the W3Schools image into the rectangle

Drag the logo from the dashed box on the right into the rectangle (it can be dragged back too).

Status: image is outside the rectangle.

2. Image Border (border-image)

border-image: ... 30 repeat;
The middle part of each edge of the image is repeated (tiled) to fill the border.

border-image: ... 30 round;
The image is repeated and scaled so a whole number of tiles fits.

border-image: ... 30 stretch;
The middle part of each edge is stretched to fill the border.
